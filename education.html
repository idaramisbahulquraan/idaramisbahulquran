Syllabus Overview

Jamia Ashrafia aims at preparing students who can preach the true version of Islam and serve the nation. The syllabus spans 15 years covering Nursery, Primary, Secondary, Alimiyat, and Fazilat levels.

Level Subjects Included
Primary Classes Holy Quran, Theology, Ethics, Sociology, Urdu, Hindi, Science, Mathematics, Geography, Urdu Grammar, Basic English, General Knowledge
Secondary Classes Persian (Grammar, Literature, Composition), Urdu Literature, Scholasticism, Islamic Fiqh, Seerat, Hadith, Arabic (Morphology, Grammar, Rhetoric, Literature), Logic, Philosophy, History, Science, Math, English, Tajweed, Computer
Alimiyat & Fazilat Dars-e-Quran, Tafsir, Hadith, Principles of Hadith/Fiqh, Inheritance Law, Belief & Scholasticism, Arabic Rhetoric, History of Islam, Arabic/English Literature, Logic, Philosophy, Polemics, Political Science, Art of Teaching

Imam Ahmad Raza Library

The central library of the institution, named after Aalahazrat Imam Ahmad Raza. It houses a rich collection of thousands of books on Tafseer, Hadith, Fiqh, History, and more. It also includes a Rare Book Collection and a Periodical Section.

Key Features:

  • Thousands of books on Islamic Sciences
  • Textbook issuance system for students
  • Maktabatul Lughatil Arabiah (Arabic Language & Literature)
  • Ashrafi Darul Mut'ala (Study Room) established in 1936
