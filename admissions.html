Required Documents

For All Applicants

  • Copy of Student's B-Form / ID Card
  • Copy of Father's CNIC
  • Two Passport-sized Photographs
  • Copy of Previous Educational Certificates/Results

Admission Process

  1. Submission: Submit the completed application form with required documents.
  2. Written Exam: Candidates must pass a written entrance exam.
  3. Interview: Successful candidates will be called for an interview with the administration.
  4. Final Selection: Admission is granted based on merit and availability of seats.
