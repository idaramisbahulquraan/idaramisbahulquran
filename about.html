Misbah ul Quran at a Glance

Overview: Idara Misbah ul Quran, located in Bahawalpur, Punjab, Pakistan, is a distinguished academic and charitable religious institution. Established in 2003 under the auspices of the "Noor e Mustafa Foundation," the institution has become a beacon of Islamic and contemporary education.

Facilities & Resources

  • Campus: Sprawling campus in New Muslim Town, Bahawalpur, with academic facilities, mosque, and hostels.
  • Libraries and Labs: Well-stocked library and modern computer lab.
  • Hostel: Comfortable living quarters for students.

Our Buildings & Campus

Our campus stretches over 55 acres, featuring magnificent structures designed for learning and spirituality.

Central Building

Two-storeyed building with 31 rooms, including a large Darul Hadith with a 107 feet high dome.

Azizul Masajid

A grand mosque stretched over 53,145 square feet, one of the greatest mosques in the region.

Hostels

Azizi Hostel (120 rooms) and Barkati Hostel (22 rooms) providing accommodation for students.

Teachers Colony

20 flats equipped with modern facilities for our dedicated faculty.

About Bahawalpur

Bahawalpur, located in the southern region of Punjab, Pakistan, is a city steeped in history and cultural vibrancy. Established in 1802 as a princely state by Nawab Bahawal Khan I, the city has evolved into an important cultural and educational hub. It is known for its striking contrast between fertile agricultural lands and the stark beauty of the nearby Cholistan Desert.
