Our Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that drive our commitment to excellence.

Our Vision

Islam is a religion of humanity that breaks the chains of superstitions and provides the light of knowledge and insight. It teaches resistance against oppression and discrimination. The educational and economic system of Islam is a complete movement against ignorance. Supporting the full utilization of scientific inventions, it addresses the heart and soul of a person, inviting them to follow divine commands and purify their heart and mind.

Our Mission

To provide the best quality education and training to all students with available resources so that they become practicing scholars, Qaris, and Huffaz of the Quran, striving for the dominance of Islam.
