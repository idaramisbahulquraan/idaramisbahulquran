Idara Misbah ul Quran: At a Glance

Idara Misbah ul Quran, located in Bahawalpur, Punjab, Pakistan, is a distinguished academic and charitable religious institution. Established in 2003 under the auspices of the “Noor e Mustafa Foundation,” the institution has become a beacon of Islamic and contemporary education. With a commitment to spreading the teachings of Islam and promoting holistic education, Idara Misbah ul Quran serves as a critical pillar in the educational and moral fabric of the region.

Our Vision

Islam is a religion of humanity that breaks the chains of superstitions and provides the light of knowledge and insight. It teaches resistance against oppression and discrimination. The educational and economic system of Islam is a complete movement against ignorance. Supporting the full utilization of scientific inventions, it addresses the heart and soul of a person, inviting them to follow divine commands and purify their heart and mind. Islam is the only remedy for the rediscovery of the modern era and the issues of the contemporary world.

Our Mission

To provide the best quality education and training to all students with available resources so that they become practicing scholars, Qaris, and Huffaz of the Quran, striving for the dominance of Islam.

Misbahi Motto

Enroll to become a practicing scholar of religion and spread the light of the Quran and Sunnah throughout the world.
